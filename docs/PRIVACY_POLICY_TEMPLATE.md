## Summary

Hanuman Chalisa does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

## Information stored locally

The app stores notes, photos selected for notes, jaap counts, sankalp progress, reading progress, and app preferences on the user's device. The native app does not upload this content to us. Deleting a note removes that note from the app. Removing the app may delete all locally stored app data.

## Photos

The app asks for photo-library permission only when a user chooses to attach a photo to a private note. Selected photos are copied into the app's local storage and are not uploaded by the native app. Permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

## Notifications

If the user enables a reminder, the app requests notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on the device. The app does not use notification permission for advertising. Permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

## Audio

The app plays a bundled devotional recording and supports background playback. It does not record audio and does not request microphone access.
